6 eggs
  1. 01

    The Konami code

    The ember field behind the site goes into overdrive for three seconds: the smoke churns five times faster and far more of it catches fire.

    1. Open the homepage, the résumé, or stay on this page.
    2. Click an empty part of the page so it has the keyboard.
    3. Type ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A.
    Try it here, the keys light up as you go
    ↑↑↓↓←→←→BA
    WhereHomepage, résumé, here
    NeedsA keyboard
    NoteWith animations off in your system settings, the embers stay still
  2. 02

    The bubble whispers

    The round cursor, the mouse bubble, turns into a little pill with a note when it passes over certain words. Some have several notes: hover again for the next one.

    1. On a computer, move the mouse slowly over headings, numbers, links and photos.
    2. When the bubble stretches into a pill, read it.

  3. 03

    The lens

    Over a few paragraphs the bubble grows into a lens, and inside it the text is different: a plainer, more honest version of what the paragraph says. Sweep it across to read the whole thing.

    1. On the homepage, hover the line under the big name, the first paragraph of About, and the research summary.
    2. Move slowly. The hidden text only shows inside the circle.
    Try it here

    This paragraph looks ordinary. Hover it with your mouse and sweep the bubble slowly from left to right, the way you would read with a magnifying glass. There you go. This is the lens. Three paragraphs on the homepage have a second version like this one, and the second version is always the honest one.

    WhereHomepage, 3 paragraphs, here
    NeedsA mouse
  4. 04

    A note in the console

    Open the developer tools and the site says hello, with a link to its source, the credits, and the terminal résumé.

    1. Press F12, or Ctrl Shift I (⌥ ⌘ I on a Mac). Right-click is switched off on this site, so the menu route won't work.
    2. Open the Console tab.
    Try it here

    This page left one for you when it loaded. Open the console now.

    WhereHomepage, résumé, certificates, 404, here
    NeedsA computer
  5. 05

    A portrait in the source

    The homepage's HTML ends with a portrait drawn in text characters, tucked in a comment after the very last tag, where only people reading the source will ever see it.

    1. Open the homepage and press Ctrl U (⌥ ⌘ U on a Mac) to view its source.
    2. Scroll all the way to the bottom.
