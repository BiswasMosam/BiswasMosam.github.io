(03) · Research

Published at IEEE

“Comparative Analysis of Psychometric Data for Assessing Mental Health Testing Needs Using Machine Learning Models”

IEEE · ICAIIHI 2025 PHQ-9 · GAD-7 · SHAP

Can supervised learning decide who should proceed to a full mental-health evaluation? We benchmarked Logistic Regression, Random Forest, SVM, XGBoost and LightGBM on psychometric responses, with cross-validation and SHAP interpretability. Logistic Regression came out near-perfect and transparent enough to be clinically meaningful for pre-screening. Plain English: we asked five models to guess who should get a full mental-health check-up. The simplest one, logistic regression, won, and it can explain every call it makes. In a clinic, explainable beats clever. Sometimes boring wins.
